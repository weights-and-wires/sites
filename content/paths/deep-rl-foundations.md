The deep RL algorithms that most later work builds on, in three families: Q-learning from DQN to Rainbow, policy optimization from the policy gradient theorem to PPO, and the off-policy actor-critics from DDPG to SAC. The path ends with how to evaluate them fairly. Assumes the basics of MDPs, value functions, and neural network training.
